# Privacy Policy - SpeedGuard

Last updated: 2026-04-14

## 1. Who we are

SpeedGuard is an Android speed monitoring application.
Privacy contact: `speedguard@111.md`.

## 2. Data we use

SpeedGuard uses:

- Location data (device location services) to calculate real-time speed
- Local app settings (thresholds, alert options, language/unit preferences)

## 3. How we use data

Data is used only to:

- display current speed
- trigger configured alerts
- run foreground monitoring correctly

## 4. Storage and sharing

- Settings are stored locally on your device.
- SpeedGuard does not sell personal data.
- SpeedGuard does not intentionally transmit your location to private backend servers.

## 5. Permissions

The app may request:

- Location permission (required for speed monitoring)
- Notification permission (required for foreground service)
- Overlay permission (optional, for visual alert over other apps)

## 6. User control

- You can stop monitoring at any time.
- You can revoke permissions in Android settings.
- You can clear local app data from Android app settings.

## 7. Children

The app is not specifically directed to children under 13.

## 8. Policy changes

This policy may be updated over time.
The "Last updated" date will reflect changes.

## 9. Contact

For privacy requests: `speedguard@111.md`
